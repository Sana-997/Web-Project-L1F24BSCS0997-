Paper & Petals, at a glance

Everyday objects. Extraordinary care.

Paper & Petals is a stationery brand for people who find joy in the handwritten and the thoughtfully made. We create premium paper goods with a botanical sensibility: soft colours, tactile materials and details that reward a closer look.

From notebooks and planners to pens, greeting cards, gift sets and washi tapes, our collections bring a little beauty to everyday rituals and a personal touch to gifting.

A few notes on using our assets

  1. Credit imagery to “Paper & Petals” and link to the brand when publishing online.
  2. Keep wordmarks, colours and product images true to the supplied originals. Please do not distort logos or apply filters.
  3. Tell us where materials will appear. For advertising, resale, extensive retouching or other commercial uses, request written permission first.

Resource requests are enquiries, not instant downloads.
