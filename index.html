A little about us

For a life beautifully written.

Paper & Petals began with a simple belief: the things we reach for every day should feel special. A fresh notebook. A handwritten hello. A plan that leaves room to dream.

We bring together tactile papers, floral-inspired details and thoughtful craftsmanship to make stationery that feels as personal as the words you put on it.
